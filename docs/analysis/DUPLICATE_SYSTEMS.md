# System authority

AquaPulse owns biological production, field observations, farm workflows and operational evidence. ERPNext owns commercial stock valuation, invoices, accounting and payments; Frappe HR/India Payroll owns payroll calculations. Integration records link those authorities. Do not maintain two independently editable accounting ledgers or treat biological head count as warehouse valuation.

The new farm hierarchy coexists with legacy ponds until an explicit mapping is approved. Do not infer responsibility or ownership from a matching name.
